Upstream numerical demand replay

The optional src/utilities/generate_demand_inputs.py retains the study's numerical
Fanger/PMV fallback and virtual-persona update rules from its archived
run_full_archive_virtual_ltpref.py. The archived routine describes the ISO 7730
numerical heat balance. Earlier study scripts called pythermalcomfort for PMV,
and the archived fallback was numerically compared with those retained outputs.
This release does not import or distribute that package.

The original 19-scene file remains an externally supplied, checksum-locked
input. Its question text, semantic descriptions and original file are not
redistributed. The optional replay exports only anonymous numerical values and
new replay labels, without original survey identifiers or identity crosswalks.
